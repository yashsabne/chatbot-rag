ABOUT RENTSMART

RentSmart is a rental platform that helps users find rental properties
and allows property owners to list and manage their properties.

ACCOUNT

Users can create an account by registering with their name, email,
and password. After registration, users can log in and manage their profile.

FINDING PROPERTIES

Users can search for properties using the property search page.
Users can use filters to narrow results based on available property
information such as location, price, and property type.

LISTING A PROPERTY

Property owners can list a property by opening the List Property section,
entering the required property details, adding images, and submitting
the listing.

BOOKING

Users can select an available property and proceed through the booking
process from the property details page.

PAYMENTS

Users can make rental or booking payments through the payment options
provided by RentSmart. Payment confirmation is displayed after a
successful transaction.

CANCELLATION

Users can cancel a booking from the booking section when cancellation
is permitted according to the applicable booking policy.

SUPPORT

Users can report problems or request help through the RentSmart support
section.

PRIVACY

RentSmart users should keep their account credentials private and should
not share their passwords with other users.
